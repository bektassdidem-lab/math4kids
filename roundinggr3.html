How to Play

1. At the top there is a target number. Example: Target 340, nearest ten.

2. In your head, round each balloon's number to the nearest ten.

3. Pop every balloon that rounds to 340! Leave the others alone and let them fly away.

💡 To round to the nearest ten, look at the ones digit. To round to the nearest hundred, look at the tens digit. If it is 5 or more, round up. If it is less than 5, round down!
🏁 In Two Teams mode the board is split in half. Both sides get the same balloons. A right balloon wins points, a wrong balloon loses a point.
